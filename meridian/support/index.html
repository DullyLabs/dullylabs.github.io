Common questions

How do I add or remove a city?

Tap + to search by city or time zone. Tap Edit to reorder cities or remove one.

How do I see the time later today?

Drag the slider at the bottom of the screen up to 12 hours either way. Tap Now to go back to the current time.

How do I choose a widget's cities?

Touch and hold the widget, tap Edit Widget, then pick its cities. Each widget keeps its own list.

Why is a band dark?

A city's band is light from 6am to 6pm there and dark the rest of the day.

Do I need an account?

No. Meridian has no sign-in and collects no data.

How do I report a problem?

Email hello@dullylabs.com. A screenshot helps.
