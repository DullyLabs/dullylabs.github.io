Meridian / Privacy

Privacy Policy

Meridian collects no data. The cities you pick stay on your device.

Effective 21 November 2026

What the app stores

Meridian saves your list of cities and their order on your device, so they are there next time you open it. Each widget keeps the cities you chose for it. Deleting the app removes all of this.

What the app collects

Nothing. Meridian does not connect to the internet, has no account, and sends no personal information, usage data, diagnostics, identifiers or location to the developer or anyone else.

Tracking and third parties

Meridian has no advertising, analytics or third-party code, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Meridian collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this changes, this page will be updated before the app does, and the date above will change.
