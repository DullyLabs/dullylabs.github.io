WEEKLY PULSE / LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Weekly Pulse is designed to keep your personal reflections personal. We do not collect your data.

Effective 25 July 2026

The short version

Your check-ins and settings are stored on your device. When iCloud is available, they sync through your own private iCloud database. The developer cannot access that database. Weekly Pulse contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Information you enter

Weekly Pulse stores the ratings, notes, pillar settings, goals, and preferences you choose to enter. This information is used only to provide the app’s features on your devices.

Storage and iCloud

Your data is stored locally using Apple’s on-device storage frameworks. When iCloud is available, Apple CloudKit syncs it through the private database associated with your Apple Account. If iCloud is unavailable, the app continues using local storage. The developer does not operate a server for Weekly Pulse and cannot read data in your private iCloud database.

Data collection and tracking

Weekly Pulse does not collect personal information, usage data, diagnostics, identifiers, or location data. It does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Notifications

If you choose to enable reminders, Weekly Pulse schedules them locally on your device. CloudKit may use Apple push notifications to keep your devices in sync. These notifications are handled by Apple’s services and are not used for marketing or tracking.

Exporting and deleting data

Data export is initiated by you and shared only with the destination you select through the system share sheet. You can delete individual entries in the app. You can also remove the app’s local data from your devices and manage its synced data through your iCloud settings.

Third parties

Weekly Pulse includes no third-party SDKs and sends no data to third-party services. Apple’s platform services, including iCloud and notifications, are governed by Apple’s own privacy policy.

Children’s privacy

Because Weekly Pulse does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s privacy practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect. The effective date above will always identify the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to weeklypulse@dulangaj.com.
