Privacy Policy

Weekly Pulse keeps your check-ins on your devices. The developer does not collect or have access to them.

Effective 25 July 2026

The short version

Your check-ins and settings are stored on your device. When iCloud is available, they sync through the private iCloud database linked to your Apple Account. The developer cannot access that database. Weekly Pulse has no advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Information you enter

The app saves the ratings, notes, pillar settings, goals, and preferences you enter. It uses this information only to provide its features on your devices.

Storage and iCloud

Your data is stored locally on your device. When iCloud is available, Apple CloudKit syncs it through the private database linked to your Apple Account. If iCloud is unavailable, the app continues to use local storage. The developer does not operate a server for Weekly Pulse and cannot read your private iCloud data.

Data collection and tracking

Weekly Pulse does not collect personal information, usage data, diagnostics, identifiers, or location data. It includes no analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Weekly Pulse schedules them on your device. CloudKit may use Apple push notifications to sync changes between your devices. These notifications are not used for marketing or tracking.

Exporting and deleting data

Weekly Pulse exports data only when you ask it to. You choose where the exported file is saved or shared. You can delete individual entries in the app, remove local data by deleting the app, and manage synced data in your iCloud settings.

Third parties

Weekly Pulse includes no third-party SDKs. It uses Apple platform services, including iCloud and notifications, which are covered by Apple’s privacy policy. It does not send data to other services.

Children’s privacy

Weekly Pulse does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not collect personal information from any user.

Changes to this policy

If these privacy practices change, this policy will be updated before the changes take effect. The effective date above shows when the policy was last revised.

Contact

For privacy questions, email weeklypulse@dulangaj.com.
