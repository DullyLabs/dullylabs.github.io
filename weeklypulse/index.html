02 · Your pillars

Use the four defaults,
or change them.

Weekly Pulse starts with Work, Play, Love, and Health. Rename any of them, recolour them, rewrite what each one means to you and the prompt you see at check-in. Add your own too, and retire the ones that stop mattering.

03 · Showing up

Close the ring.
Keep the streak.

Set a weekly goal you can hit. The ring closes when you reach it. A streak is weeks in a row of closing it, so you can't lose one mid-week, only by finishing under target. It counts whether you turned up, never how high you scored.

🔥 26 weeks. A broken streak is meant to be recoverable, not demoralising.

04 · Private by design

There's no server.
So there's nothing to leak.

Your check-ins are stored on your device. Turn on iCloud and they sync through your own private iCloud database. Nobody else can read it, including me. Export everything whenever you like. It's your data.
