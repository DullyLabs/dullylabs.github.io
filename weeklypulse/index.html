01 / THE IDEA

“A good life isn’t a score to perfect. It’s a pattern to notice.”

Take a minute each week to record how life feels. Over time, Weekly Pulse helps the signal emerge—without feeds, streak pressure, or someone else’s definition of balance.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

Four parts of life.

01

Check in

Rate your four personal pillars in a few taps. Add a note when there is more to remember.

02

03

See the shape

Follow changes across weeks, months, and years. Notice where your attention has been—and where it might go next.
