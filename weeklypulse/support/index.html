WEEKLY PULSE / SUPPORT

How can I help?

If Weekly Pulse is not behaving as expected, send a note. Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Email support

A few useful answers

Do I need an account?

No. Weekly Pulse works without an account or sign-in. Optional sync uses the Apple Account already configured on your device.

Where is my data stored?

Your entries are stored on your device. When iCloud is available, they are also stored in your private iCloud database so they can stay in sync across your devices.

Can the developer see my check-ins?

No. There is no Weekly Pulse server, analytics service, or developer dashboard containing your entries.

How do I export my data?

Open Settings in Weekly Pulse and use the export option. The app creates a file that you control through Apple’s standard share sheet.

How do I report a problem?

Email weeklypulse@dulangaj.com. Screenshots are helpful, but please remove anything personal before attaching them.
