Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. You can use Weekly Pulse without creating an account. If iCloud is available, sync uses the Apple Account signed in on your device.

Where is my data stored?

Your entries are stored on your device. When iCloud is available, they also sync through your private iCloud database.

Can the developer see my check-ins?

No. Weekly Pulse has no developer-run server or analytics service. Your entries are not available to me.

How do I export my data?

Open Settings in Weekly Pulse and choose the export option. The app creates a file that you can save or share using the system share sheet.

How do I report a problem?

Email weeklypulse@dulangaj.com. If you attach screenshots, remove any personal information first.
