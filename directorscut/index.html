Demo pacing · 2× speed

Your iPhone runs the camera.

Put it on a tripod. Director's Cut films with two lenses and cuts between them live, like a broadcast director.

Two lenses at once

The wide and tele lenses record together. You get the edited program, a file per lens, and a cut list.

Cuts that wait

It holds wide, waits for the speaker to settle, then zooms. No shot runs too long.

The face stays in

If a face nears the edge, it cuts wide at once. The body may crop; the face may not.

Also saves the best stills from the talk, picked for you.
