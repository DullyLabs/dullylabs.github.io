Common questions

Which iPhones does it work on?

It needs an iPhone with both a wide and a telephoto rear camera that can record from both at once. It films in landscape.

Where do my recordings go?

When you stop recording, the edited video and the stills it picked are saved to your Photos library. The working files are then removed from the app.

Saving to Photos failed. Is my recording lost?

No. If the save fails, the app keeps the session folder. Allow photo additions for Director's Cut in the Settings app; you can also find the files in the Files app under On My iPhone › Director's Cut.

Can I turn off the stills?

Yes. Open Settings in the app and turn off Keep Stills, or change how many stills it keeps.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in, and the app does not connect to any server.

How do I report a problem?

Email hello@dullylabs.com. A session log helps: open Settings › Logs in the app, pick the recording, and tap Export.
