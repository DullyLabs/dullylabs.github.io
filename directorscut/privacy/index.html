Privacy Policy

Director's Cut records and edits on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Effective 12 October 2026

The short version

The app has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no third-party code. It does not connect to the internet. Your videos and photos go to your own Photos library, and the developer never sees them.

Camera and microphone

Director's Cut uses both rear cameras and the microphone to record your talk. The preview is shown live on screen; nothing is saved until you press record.

Face and body detection

While it films, the app uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to find the speaker in each frame. That is how it picks a shot and scores stills. This happens on your iPhone, is used only during the recording, and is not stored.

Motion

The app reads the phone's motion sensor to tell how level the tripod is, so it can straighten the stills. The reading is not stored.

Photos

When a recording ends, the app asks to add to your Photos library and saves the edited video and the stills it picked. It asks for add-only access, so it cannot see the rest of your library. If you use iCloud Photos, Apple syncs them like any other photo.

Files on your iPhone

During a recording the app keeps working files in its own folder: the edited video, one video per lens, and a list of the cuts. After a successful save to Photos it deletes them. If the save fails, it keeps them so the recording is not lost; you can see them in the Files app.

Each recording also writes a short technical log, such as cut and still counts. Logs stay on your iPhone. You can read or delete them in Settings › Logs, and they leave the device only if you tap Export.

Settings

Your choices in the app's Settings are stored on your iPhone.

Deleting your data

Delete videos and stills in Photos. Delete logs in Settings › Logs. Deleting the app removes everything else it stored.

Children's privacy

Director's Cut does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's privacy practices change, this page will be updated first. The date above shows the last revision.
