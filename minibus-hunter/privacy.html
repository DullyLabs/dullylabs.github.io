What the app keeps

Your pinned stops and preferences are stored on your device and, if you have iCloud turned on, synced through your personal iCloud. The developer never sees any of it.

App Privacy label

Data Not Collected. The app’s privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data. No account, no ads, no selling of personal data.

Arrival data

Arrival data from the Transport Department, via DATA.GOV.HK. The app requests it for the stops you view; those requests don’t identify you.
