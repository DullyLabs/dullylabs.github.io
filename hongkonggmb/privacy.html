Your stops
stay yours.

What the app keeps

Your pinned stops and preferences are stored on your device and, if you have iCloud turned on, synced through your personal iCloud. The developer never sees any of it.

What we don’t do

No accounts, no advertising, no selling of personal data.

Arrival data

To show arrivals, the app requests public transport data for the stops you view. Those requests don’t identify you.
